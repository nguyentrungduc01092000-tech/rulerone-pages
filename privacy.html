Privacy

This store uses browser storage for favorites and a preview cart on your device. It does not collect card details. Enabled purchases use the payment provider checkout. For orders arranged directly with the store, the administrator may record the customer email, optional name, purchased products, payment status and a support note for fulfillment and customer support. Those records are stored privately on this website, together with payment references. Store administration uses ChatGPT sign-in; the hosting service processes sign-in information and request logs to operate the site. Administrator identity is checked on the server. Images and free sample PDFs on published listings are public. Full product download files are private. For a confirmed paid order, the administrator can create a time-limited download link and share it directly with the customer. Anyone with that link can use it until it expires, reaches its limit or is revoked. You can clear favorites and the cart using the store controls or your browser settings. Support requests submitted through the contact form store your email, optional order reference, message, status and submission time privately on this website. This information is used to troubleshoot your issue and respond. Email correspondence is processed by the email provider you use. Contact details will be displayed when the store owner adds a support email.

When you buy through Lemon Squeezy, that service processes checkout, payment and receipt delivery. Verified order details, purchase email, product, amount, currency and refund status are also recorded privately on this website for sales history and support. The website does not collect your card details. Checkout references contain product and file identifiers and are kept for up to 90 days. Payment service terms and privacy information are displayed at checkout.

Store statistics count page views, product views, additions to the preview cart and clicks on free sample links. Only daily totals and product identifiers are stored, for up to 90 days; the analytics feature does not store your IP address, email, browser fingerprint or visitor identifier, and uses no analytics cookies. It respects browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals. Counts may include repeat visits and automated traffic; sample clicks do not confirm a completed download. Hosting request logs are separate from these statistics.
